## 終態
**測一不通過｜不進 T。** 機制之存在與入帳方式於一手申報成立：FY25 20-F 明寫「voluntary marketplace programs that allow eligible artists, labels, and distributors … to sponsor on-platform recommendations. Some of these programs apply a discounted royalty rate to streams generated in specified recommendation contexts」，且「Cost of revenue also reflects discounts provided by certain rights holders in return for promotional activities in connection with marketplace programs」（FY21 起逐字同句）。但 **(2) 觸發（先驗）**：FY21–3Q26 共讀 6 份申報，Premium 段之 marketplace 效益**從未單獨量化**，一律與營收成長、有聲書、Partner Program 併為一個淨額（3Q26 €353M、9M26 €833M、FY25 €765M、FY24 €1,078M、FY23 €814M）；唯一含 marketplace 之數字在 Ad-Supported 段且仍為「primarily」或「net of」併列（FY25 €21M 降幅「due primarily to」；2Q26 €8M「net of certain marketplace programs」）。折讓率 d 無一手：Spotify 方案主頁 404，contexts 頁不列費率；「30%」只見於 2024 單一藝人部落格與 2026 廠商部落格之轉述。s_DM 無任何來源。`[D]` 份額區間無可界定下界與上界，寬於 [0, 50%] 之一半。(3) 部分觸發、(4) 部分觸發、(1)(6) 未觸發、(5) 未判定。
**判讀：** 誘因結構有一手自承（FY23 Premium 毛利率上升「due primarily to benefits from certain marketplace programs」），但其占毛利擴張之份額不可分離——與 L11-I#2–#4 同型死因。不進 T；由複查觸發器重開。
