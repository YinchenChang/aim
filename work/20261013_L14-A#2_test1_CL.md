## B. 次級路徑
- ASU 2025-06 提前採用：WebSearch 候選 8 份 10-Q 中實讀 5 份非科技（Hershey、O'Reilly、First United、Liberty Latin America、Superior Group）＋ UPS／JPM／Citi／PepsiCo：**無一提前採用**。O'Reilly（[10-Q 2Q26](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0000898173/000089817326000045/orly-20260630x10q.htm)）「will adopt this guidance beginning with its first quarter ending March 31, 2028 … not expected to have a material impact」；UPS 10-Q 2Q26 就本 ASU：「We are evaluating the impact of adoption, but do not expect this ASU to have a significant impact on our consolidated financial position, results of operations, cash flows or internal controls」。PepsiCo 原文：「disclosures for property, plant and equipment will be required for all capitalized software costs」——生效後（2028）可見端改善。
- 賣方／評論：S&P Global MI（[連結](https://www.spglobal.com/market-intelligence/en/news-insights/research/ai-savings-could-lead-to-upward-earnings-surprises-in-tech)，2025-07-04）「Cost savings from AI-driven efficiencies are rarely reflected in software companies' profitability forecasts」——對象為軟體公司，未涉資本化。另一檢索（capitalized software × AI × earnings quality）未見對非科技買方之資本化分列量化。
