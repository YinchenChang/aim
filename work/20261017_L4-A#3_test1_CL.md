## 終態
**測一不通過｜不進 T。** (2)(6) 觸發；(1)(4) 部分；(3)(5) 未觸發。
**理由：** ① **卡之核心反身性（股價↑ → 收入減項↑）被現行 GAAP 明文切斷**：ASC 606-10-32-25A 要求對客戶之股份對價「shall be measured at the grant date in accordance with Topic 718」，「Changes in the measurement … that are due to the form of the consideration shall not be included in the transaction price」，「This includes changes to the fair-value-based measure of liability-classified awards」（Deloitte DART §14.6）。故 AMD 權證雖為負債分類，授予後之股價變動只能入「elsewhere in the grantor's income statement」（非收入）；收入減項＝授予日公允價值（含股價門檻之市場條件）× 歸屬機率，按 GPU 收入認列比例沖減——一經授予即與日後股價無關。② **前例路徑不可比**：Plug Power 10-K/A FY2020 明言採用 ASU 2019-08 後「the amount recorded as a reduction of revenue was measured based on the grant-date fair value of the warrants. Previously, this amount was measured based on vesting date fair value」——卡所引之「股價連動收入減項」只存在於 ASU 2019-08 之前。③ 剩餘之「股價 → 損益」連動在營業外重評價，屬另一機制（非收入計量、且 AMD 法說「refer primarily to non-GAAP」），本卡不涵蓋；記為複查觸發器。
