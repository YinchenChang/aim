## A. 指名 B 端實讀

**A-1 電網端數值條款（(1) 第二支：已取得）**
- ERCOT SPWG 簡報（2026-02，NOGRR282／NPRR1308 條文版）https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/03/02/04_LEL-RT-Requirements_SPWG_Feb2026.pdf ：LEL＝「A Large Load in which 50% or greater of the Demand at the site consists of power electronic based load」；Large Load ≥ 75 MW。頻率：58.8 ≤ f ≤ 61.2 Hz「Continuous」；57.0 ≤ f < 58.8「299 seconds」。電壓：0.80 ≤ V < 0.90「2.0 seconds」；0.50–0.80「0.5 seconds」；0.20–0.50「0.25 seconds」；V < 0.20「0.15 seconds」。「shall return to 90% of its pre-disturbance consumption level from the grid within two seconds」；電流上限 125%（簡報註：實測事件 150–200%）。此為核准前條文版；PUCT 核准版（https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/07/11/282NOGRR-45-PUCT-Report-070926.docx ）未能讀出。
- ERCOT 董事會簡報（2026-09-15 修訂）https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/09/17/10-Interconnection-and-Grid-Analysis-Update-REVISED.pdf ：「The PUCT approved NOGRR 282/ NPRR 1308 in July, effective on August 1」；草擬 NOGRR「will recommend a limit of 10 MW per 5 seconds for power variation in Large Loads」，「plans to submit NOGRR in September or October」；「Certain data centers processes can oscillate in a way that damages grid equipment」。
- ERCOT LLWG（2026-06-19）https://www.ercot.com/files/docs/2026/06/19/ERCOT-LCL-Power-Variation-Limit-Discussion_fin.pdf ：「shall not exceed 10 MW over any rolling 5-second interval」；量測保留「0.1 Hz to 55.0 Hz」。LLWG 4-23 摘要 https://www.zeroemissiongrid.com/iso-rto/ercot-llwg-4-23/ ：「ERCOT confirmed that the 10 MW / 5-second limit will apply to existing loads as well」。
- NERC Level 3 Alert 原文（2026-05-04；NYSRC 轉載）https://www.nysrc.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/NYSRC-LLWG-5-Att.-5-NERC-Level-3-Alert-1.pdf ：七項 Essential Actions；適用「20 MW and greater, connected at 60kV, and contain more than 1 MW of IT Load」（草擬註冊）；要求申報「expected maximum ramp rate (MW/min)」但**無數值上限**；無振盪頻帶數值；報告期限 2026-08-03。
