- **(3) 部分觸發。** S&P《Corporate Methodology: Ratios And Adjustments》（2013-11-19 存檔版，[maalot.co.il](https://maalot.co.il/Publications/MT20191103145619.PDF)）：「We include restructuring costs in our calculation of EBITDA」，理由「most companies need to restructure at some point」——評等端以含重組口徑計；現行 2019 版未取得。賣方以多年均值回加：未檢得具名原文（非不存在）。研究端：Griffin & McInnis 2025（[IDEAS](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jaecon/v80y2025i1s0165410125000357.html)）樣本仍非重組，未檢得涵蓋重組之具名研究。監理端：SEC 曾以 C&DI 100.01 就重組排除項發意見函（[Mercury Systems CORRESP 2017](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1049521/000104952117000002/filename1.htm)「Please explain to us why these are not normal, recurring, cash operating expenses」；[CORRESP 2016](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1024305/000119312516734726/filename1.htm) 同語），發行人回覆「not normal and recurring」後續保留——R2 之自我節制未見，惟非本卡否決條件。
- **(4) 未觸發。** 樞紐為重組行／滾存／橋接，異於 IT 支出比、費用化／資本化、理由文字、席次與單位經濟。
